08 / BEFORE YOU ASK

Worth knowing.
Before we talk.

Can this test my AI agent?+

The product supports reference agents and authorized staging agents through compatible connectors. Test depth depends on agent access and tool visibility.

Is the data synthetic?+

Yes. The browser demos use fictional records. The local platform can also generate UAE/KSA-themed test fixtures.

Is the demo a real AI audit?+

No. The browser demo tests a deterministic reference agent. Live-agent assessments require authorized staging access and analyst review.

Can you provide AI insurance?+

No. SIYAJ does not sell, arrange, broker or underwrite insurance. We develop technical AI-risk evidence that may inform conversations with properly licensed insurers and brokers.

10 / PRIVACY & PUBLIC DEMOS

Your data stays yours.

Interactive demonstrations: The red-team simulator, synthetic-data generator and pilot-scope builder run in your browser. They use fictional content and do not submit data to a real AI agent.

Inquiries: Without an external form endpoint, the inquiry is copied locally and no message is sent. If the site owner enables a form provider, submitted information may be transmitted to that provider; the owner must publish its identity, retention and privacy details before enabling it.

Safety: Please do not enter passwords, confidential prompts, real customer records or other sensitive materials. This demonstration website intentionally contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

Pre-launch privacy summary. The site owner must complete the legally required privacy notice and contact details before accepting live inquiries.
